# TrustLock — Demo Voiceover Script

**Target runtime:** ≤ 2:40 (video limit: 3:00)
**Style:** Deep, resonant, commanding male narrator — rich cinematic texture. Confident, steady, professional. Gravitas and suspense, articulate crystal-clear cadence. High-end tech documentary / dramatic film trailer.

**Voice settings (if using ElevenLabs or similar):**
- Stability: **45–55%** (natural, expressive storytelling flow — not robotic, not erratic)
- Clarity / Similarity: **75–85%** (sharp, clean, professional articulation)
- Style Exaggeration: **15–25%** (a touch of cinematic drama, never forced)

**Production notes:** Pause ~1s at each scene cut. Let the honesty beat (2:15) land in near-silence after "stays held." No music under the AI-verification section — let the trace speak. End cold on "verified."
